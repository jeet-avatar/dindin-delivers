# 04 — Video Scripts (Short-form + YouTube walkthrough)

Style: **MIX**. Mostly faceless screen recordings of Ableton, BeatMind or MixMind, with voiceover (VO) or on-screen text, plus a few hands/decks/founder-voice clips. Every script is 9:16 (1080×1920) unless noted.

**Ground rules for every video**
1. **Show real output.** Record real sessions only. If you speed something up, put a small `sped up 2×` or `real time 0:47` label in a corner. The honesty is part of the brand.
2. **No mock UI.** If a feature doesn't work on camera, cut it. Don't fake it.
3. **Audio = our own.** The music in each video is the loop you just built with BeatMind, or a track you have the rights to. Don't use copyrighted trending audio. On a TikTok Business account you only get the Commercial Music Library anyway (see `01-handles-and-accounts.md`). For MixMind videos that show a DJ library, **mute the track audio** or use your own/licensed tracks. Library *titles* on screen are fine, but blur any you don't want public.
4. **Captions burned in** (most people watch muted), plus platform auto-captions switched on.
5. **CTA** is always one of these: "link in bio", "7-day free trial, 3 tracks, no card", or "beatmind.io". Pick one per video. **MixMind videos:** use "link in bio" or "MixMind from $12/mo". Never say MixMind has a free trial (the trial is BeatMind only).

Legend: **[SC]** = screen capture · **[H]** = hands/controller/decks footage · **[V]** = founder voice

## Recording tips

### Before you hit record
- macOS **Focus / Do Not Disturb** on. Quit Slack, Mail and Messages. Hide the dock. Clean the desktop.
- Ableton: **Preferences → Display & Input (Live 12) / Look/Feel (Live 11) → Zoom 125–150%** so the UI is readable on a phone. Use a high-contrast theme.
- Enable cursor highlight: OBS plugin, or Accessibility → Display → Pointer → size +1.
- Use a **clean demo account** and a **demo Live Set**. No personal emails or unreleased client work on screen.

### OBS Studio (recommended, captures app audio on macOS 13+)
- **Source:** "macOS Screen Capture" (ScreenCaptureKit) → Window or Display. It can capture app audio with no extra driver.
- **Horizontal master:** Settings → Video → Base (canvas) **2560×1440** (or your Retina size), Output **1920×1080**, **60 fps** (UI motion looks smoother). Use 30 fps if your machine drops frames while Live is running.
- **Vertical:** make a second scene or profile with Base/Output **1080×1920**. Place the Live window at scale and crop so the clip grid + chat fill the frame. (Or use the Aitum Vertical plugin to record both at once.)
- **Output → Recording:** format **MKV** (crash-safe), remux to MP4 afterwards. Encoder: Apple VT H.264 hardware. Rate control CBR **20–40 Mbps** for 1080p60 (or CRF/CQ ~18–20). Keyframe interval 2 s.
- **Audio:** 48 kHz. Record the mic on track 2 separately so you can mix VO later.

### QuickTime (quick and dirty)
- File → New Screen Recording → Options → pick the mic. **QuickTime can't record system/Ableton audio by itself.** Either resample the loop in Live and lay it in during editing, or route through a loopback driver (BlackHole/Loopback).
- Records at native Retina resolution. Crop to 9:16 in the editor.

### Editing
- Timeline: **1080×1920, 30 or 60 fps**. Match the source frame rate.
- **Captions:** burned in (CapCut auto-captions or Descript), Poppins SemiBold, inside the safe zone. Keep text out of the top ~220 px and bottom ~420 px of the 1920 frame (platform UI). On YouTube, also upload an SRT.
- **Loudness:** mix to about **−14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP true peak**. YouTube normalizes to about −14. Social apps are similar. VO sits clearly above the music (the music about 6–10 dB under the VO).
- **Label speed-ups** ("2×") and keep the real-time reveal where it counts. It's the proof.
- **Export:** H.264 MP4, 1080×1920, 30/60 fps, 10–16 Mbps, AAC 320 kbps 48 kHz.
- **First frame = the hook text.** No logo intro.
