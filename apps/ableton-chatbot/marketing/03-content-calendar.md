## Part A — The first 9 Instagram grid posts (profile looks complete on Day 1)

Instagram shows the **newest post top-left**, so you post in reverse order: **G1 first (it ends up bottom-right), G9 last (top-left).**
The profile grid has shown **3:4 tiles since Jan 2025**. **Design static/carousel posts at 1080×1440 (3:4)** so they fill both the feed and the grid tile with no crop. If you use 1080×1350 (4:5), the grid trims about 34 px from each side (visible ≈1013×1350), so keep text 60 px in from the left and right edges. For Reel covers (1080×1920), keep the title inside the center 1080×1440 band.

**Visual rhythm:** orange-accent (`#ff6b00`) text tiles alternate with dark screen-recording tiles in a checkerboard (◼ = orange/text tile, ▢ = dark screenshot/reel):

```
 Top row      G9 ▢ Hero reel      G8 ◼ "3 prompts"      G7 ▢ MixMind dupes reel
 Middle row   G6 ◼ "What it can't do" G5 ▢ Warmer reel  G4 ◼ How it works
 Bottom row   G3 ▢ MixMind intro  G2 ◼ Meet BeatMind    G1 ▢ Setup reel
```

**Posting schedule, Day 1:** G1–G3 in the first 60 minutes (see README). G4–G6 around midday. G7–G9 in the evening, **with G9 (the hero reel) last so it's the top-left post and the one new visitors see first.** Then **pin** G9, G2 and G6 (pinned posts sit in the top row).
