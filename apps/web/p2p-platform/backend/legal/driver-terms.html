Important: This agreement governs your relationship with Dollor.ai as an independent contractor. Please read carefully before accepting delivery or rideshare requests.

1. Nature of Relationship

1.1 Independent Contractor Status

You are an independent contractor, NOT an employee of Dollor.ai or Zietra Technologies Inc. This means:

  • You control when, where, and how long you work
  • You may accept or decline any delivery or ride request
  • You may work for competing platforms simultaneously
  • You are responsible for your own taxes, insurance, and expenses

1.2 Platform Role

Dollor.ai operates as a peer-to-peer matchmaking platform that connects you with customers seeking delivery or transportation services. We do not:

  • Control your schedule or require minimum hours
  • Provide vehicles or equipment
  • Set your prices (you bid on rideshare requests)
  • Supervise your work methods

3. Requirements

3.1 Eligibility

  • Be at least 18 years old (21 for rideshare)
  • Have a valid driver's license
  • Have valid auto insurance meeting state minimums
  • Pass a background check
  • Have a vehicle that meets our safety standards

3.2 Ongoing Obligations

  • Maintain valid license and insurance at all times
  • Keep your vehicle in safe operating condition
  • Complete deliveries/rides in a professional manner
  • Follow all applicable laws and regulations
  • Maintain a customer rating above 4.0 stars

4. Conduct Standards

4.1 You Agree To:

  • Treat customers, restaurant staff, and others with respect
  • Handle food and packages with care
  • Arrive promptly for pickups
  • Communicate professionally through the app
  • Follow contactless delivery instructions when requested

4.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following may result in immediate deactivation:
  • Harassment or discrimination of any kind
  • Operating under the influence of drugs or alcohol
  • Unsafe driving practices
  • Fraud or theft
  • Sharing customer information
  • Violating customer privacy

5. Insurance & Liability

5.1 Your Insurance

You must maintain personal auto insurance that meets or exceeds state minimum requirements. You are responsible for ensuring your policy covers commercial/rideshare activity.

5.2 Liability

As an independent contractor, you are responsible for:

  • Your own actions while providing services
  • Damage to your vehicle
  • Traffic violations and parking tickets
  • Any claims arising from your negligence

6. Account Deactivation

6.1 Grounds for Deactivation

  • Violation of these terms
  • Falling below minimum rating threshold
  • Failed background check rerun
  • Insurance lapse
  • Safety concerns
  • Fraudulent activity

6.2 Appeal Process

If your account is deactivated, you may appeal by contacting support@dollor.ai within 30 days. We will review your case and respond within 10 business days.
