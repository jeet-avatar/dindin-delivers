Welcome to Dollor.ai! This agreement outlines the terms of your partnership with our platform. By listing your restaurant, you agree to these terms.

1. Platform Overview

1.1 What We Do

Dollor.ai is a peer-to-peer marketplace that connects your restaurant with customers seeking food delivery. We provide:

  • Mobile apps for customers to discover and order from your restaurant
  • Order management tools via our Restaurant Partner app
  • Access to independent delivery drivers (or use your own)
  • Payment processing and settlement services
  • Customer support for delivery issues

1.2 Our Fee Structure

Save More with Dollor.ai! Unlike other platforms that charge 15-30% commission, we charge a flat $1 per order.

2. Your Obligations

2.1 Menu & Pricing

  • Provide accurate menu items, descriptions, and pricing
  • Keep menu availability updated in real-time
  • Honor all prices displayed on the platform
  • Clearly mark allergens and dietary information
  • Update menu within 24 hours of any changes

2.2 Order Fulfillment

  • Accept or decline orders within 3 minutes
  • Prepare orders within the estimated time provided
  • Ensure food quality and proper packaging
  • Have orders ready for driver pickup
  • Maintain food safety standards

2.3 Operating Hours

  • Keep your operating hours accurate on the platform
  • Update hours for holidays or special closures in advance
  • Process orders only during your published hours

3. Delivery Options

3.1 Platform Drivers

By default, orders are fulfilled by independent drivers on our platform. You will have 3 minutes after marking an order "Ready for Pickup" to decide:

  • Send to Drivers: Our platform will match with available drivers
  • Self-Deliver: Use your own staff for delivery

3.2 Self-Delivery

If you choose to deliver orders yourself:

  • You keep the full delivery fee paid by the customer
  • You are responsible for timely delivery
  • You must update order status in the app
  • Customer ratings will reflect on your restaurant

4. Payments & Settlement

4.1 Payment Processing

We process all customer payments through Stripe. Funds are held until orders are completed.

4.2 Settlement Schedule

Daily Settlement Orders completed by 11:59 PM settle next business day
Bank Deposit 1-2 business days after settlement

4.3 Deductions

The following are deducted from your payout:

  • $1.00 marketplace fee per order

  • Chargebacks (when applicable)

5.1 Food Safety
