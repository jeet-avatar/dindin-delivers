1. Introduction

1.1 Commitment to Privacy

Dollor.ai, Inc. ("Dollor.ai," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who use our transportation network company platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information.

1.2 Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The Dollor.ai mobile application (iOS and Android)
  • The Dollor.ai website (dollor.ai)
  • All related services, features, and content (collectively, the "Platform")

1.3 Legal Framework

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:

  • Wyoming Consumer Protection Act (W.S. 40-12-101 et seq.)
  • Wyoming Data Breach Notification Law (W.S. 40-12-502)
  • Wyoming Genetic Data Privacy Act (where applicable)
  • Federal laws including CCPA (for California residents)
  • Stripe Privacy Policy (for payment processing)

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Account Registration:

  • Full legal name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Profile photograph (optional)
  • Password (stored in hashed format)

Rider Information:

  • Saved addresses (home, work, favorites)
  • Payment card information (processed by Stripe)
  • Ride preferences

Driver Information:

  • Driver's license number and expiration date
  • Vehicle information (make, model, year, color, license plate)
  • Insurance information (carrier, policy number, coverage amounts)
  • Banking information for payouts (processed by Stripe)
  • Background check authorization
  • Tax identification (SSN for 1099 reporting)
  • Direct payment information (Venmo, Zelle, CashApp usernames - optional)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Location Data:

  • GPS location (when app is in use)
  • Pickup and dropoff locations for rides
  • Route information during rides
  • Driver location during active ride periods

Device Information:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • Mobile network information
  • IP address

3.1 Providing Services

  • Matching Riders with Drivers
  • Processing ride requests and payments
  • Providing navigation and route information
  • Enabling communication between Riders and Drivers

5. Data Security

5.1 Security Measures

Technical Safeguards:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Secure password hashing (bcrypt)
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Firewall and intrusion detection systems

5.2 Data Breach Notification

Pursuant to Wyoming Statutes Section 40-12-502, in the event of a security breach involving personal identifying information:

  • We will conduct a prompt investigation
  • If misuse is likely, we will notify affected Wyoming residents as soon as possible
  • If more than 500 Wyoming residents are affected, we will notify the Wyoming Attorney General

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Correction

You may:

  • Access your personal information through the app
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing privacy@dollor.ai
  • Correct inaccurate information in your account settings
  • Update your contact and payment information

6.2 Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and personal information by:

  • Using the "Delete Account" feature in the app
  • Emailing privacy@dollor.ai

Note: Some information may be retained for legal/regulatory compliance.

7. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will delete it promptly. Parents may contact privacy@dollor.ai if they believe their child's information was collected.

8. California Residents (CCPA Notice)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of personal information collected, used, and shared
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information (subject to exceptions)
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising CCPA rights
