## 1. PLATFORM FEE STRUCTURE

### 1.1 Distance-Based Tiered Pricing

Dollor.ai charges a **distance-based platform fee** for connecting riders with drivers. This fee is transparent and disclosed before ride confirmation per Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-103.

| Tier | Trip Distance | Platform Fee | Description |
|------|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Tier 1** | 0 - 9.99 miles | **$1.00** | Local trips within city limits |
| **Tier 2** | 10 - 24.99 miles | **$2.00** | Regional trips between nearby cities |
| **Tier 3** | 25+ miles | **$3.00** | Long-distance trips |

### 1.2 Fee Transparency

Per Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-103 (Fare Collected for Services):
- Platform fees are disclosed **BEFORE** ride confirmation
- Fees are displayed **separately** from the transportation fare
- The total charge breakdown is shown on the electronic receipt

## 2. HOW FEES ARE CALCULATED

### 2.1 Example: Local Ride (Tier 1)

**Route:** Downtown Cheyenne to Cheyenne Regional Airport (8.5 miles)

```
FARE BREAKDOWN:
  Base Fare:              $2.50
  Distance (8.5 mi × $1.15):  $9.78
  Time (15 min × $0.18):      $2.70
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Subtotal (Driver Fare):    $14.98
  Platform Fee (Tier 1):      $1.00
  ─────────────────────────────────
  TOTAL CHARGED:             $15.98

  Driver Receives:           $14.98 (100% of fare)
  Platform Receives:          $1.00 (connection fee)
```

### 2.2 Example: Regional Ride (Tier 2)

**Route:** Cheyenne to Laramie (48 miles)

```
FARE BREAKDOWN:
  Base Fare:               $2.50
  Distance (48 mi × $1.15):   $55.20
  Time (52 min × $0.18):       $9.36
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Subtotal (Driver Fare):     $67.06
  Platform Fee (Tier 3):       $3.00
  ─────────────────────────────────
  TOTAL CHARGED:              $70.06

  Driver Receives:            $67.06 (100% of fare)
  Platform Receives:           $3.00 (connection fee)
```

### 2.3 Example: Airport Pickup (with Airport Fee)

**Route:** Jackson Hole Airport to Teton Village (8.5 miles)

```
FARE BREAKDOWN:
  Base Fare:               $2.50
  Distance (8.5 mi × $1.15):   $9.78
  Time (18 min × $0.18):       $3.24
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Subtotal (Driver Fare):     $15.52
  Platform Fee (Tier 1):       $1.00
  Airport Fee (JAC):           $2.00
  ─────────────────────────────────

  Driver Receives:            $15.52 (100% of fare)
  Platform Receives:           $1.00 (connection fee)
  Airport Authority:           $2.00 (airport access fee)
```

## 3. AIRPORT FEES

Certain Wyoming airports charge access fees for TNC pickups/dropoffs:

| Airport Code | Airport Name | Fee |
|--------------|--------------|-----|
| JAC | Jackson Hole Airport | $2.00 |
| CYS | Cheyenne Regional Airport | $1.50 |
| CPR | Casper-Natrona County International | $1.50 |
| RIW | Riverton Regional Airport | $1.00 |
| SHR | Sheridan County Airport | $1.00 |
| Other | All other locations | $0.00 |

These fees are collected by Dollor.ai and remitted to the respective airport authorities.

## 4. COMPARISON TO INDUSTRY RATES

| Platform | Typical Commission | Dollor.ai Fee |
|----------|-------------------|---------------|
| Uber | 25-30% of fare | $1-$3 flat |
| Lyft | 20-28% of fare | $1-$3 flat |
| **Dollor.ai** | **$1-$3 flat** | **1.4-4.3% of fare** |

**Example:** On a $70 fare:
- Uber takes: ~$17.50-$21.00 (25-30%)
- Lyft takes: ~$14.00-$19.60 (20-28%)
- **Dollor.ai takes: $3.00 (4.3%)**

**Driver keeps more with Dollor.ai.**

## 5. DRIVER EARNINGS

### 5.1 What Drivers Receive

Drivers receive **100% of the transportation fare**. The platform fee is charged separately to the customer and does not reduce driver earnings.

### 5.2 Driver Earnings Examples

| Fare | Platform Fee | Driver Receives | Driver % |
|------|--------------|-----------------|----------|
| $15.00 | $1.00 | $15.00 | 100% |
| $35.00 | $1.00 | $35.00 | 100% |
| $50.00 | $2.00 | $50.00 | 100% |
| $75.00 | $3.00 | $75.00 | 100% |
| $100.00 | $3.00 | $100.00 | 100% |

### 5.3 Tips

- 100% of tips go directly to drivers
- Tips are not subject to platform fees
- Tips are processed through the app or paid directly

## 6. PAYMENT PROCESSING

### 6.1 How Payments Work

1. **Customer pays total** (fare + platform fee) via Stripe
2. **Driver receives fare** via direct deposit (weekly)
3. **Platform retains platform fee** ($1-$3)
4. **Airport fees** remitted to airport authorities

### 6.2 Payment Timeline

- Rides completed Monday-Sunday
- Driver payouts processed: Following Monday
- Funds available: 1-2 business days after processing

---

## 7. REFUNDS AND CANCELLATIONS

### 7.1 Cancellation Fees

| When Cancelled | Fee |
|----------------|-----|
| Before driver accepts | $0 (free) |
| After driver accepts, before pickup | $3.00 |
| After pickup begins | Full fare charged |

### 7.2 Refund Policy

Refunds may be issued for:
- Driver no-shows
- Safety concerns
- Service issues
- App malfunctions

Platform fees are refundable if the ride does not complete.
