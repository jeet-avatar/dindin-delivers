2. Definitions

  • "Digital Network" - Any online-enabled application, software, website, or system offered or used by Dollor.ai that enables riders to prearrange rides with drivers.
  • "Driver" - An independent contractor who has been authorized by Dollor.ai to access the Platform, uses a personal vehicle to provide prearranged rides to Riders, and receives compensation for providing such rides.
  • "Personal Vehicle" - A vehicle that is used by a Driver to provide prearranged rides and is owned, leased, or otherwise authorized for use by the Driver; not a taxicab, limousine, for-hire vehicle, or commercial vehicle.
  • "Prearranged Ride" - Transportation provided by a Driver to a Rider that begins when a Driver accepts a ride request through the Platform, continues while the Driver transports the Rider, and ends when the Rider exits the vehicle.
  • "Platform Fee" - The fee charged by Dollor.ai for facilitating the connection between Riders and Drivers through the Platform.
  • "Rider" - An individual who uses the Platform to request prearranged rides from Drivers.

Pursuant to Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-110, Drivers are independent contractors, NOT employees of Dollor.ai.

This applies when ALL of the following conditions are met:

  • Dollor.ai does not prescribe specific hours during which you must be available
  • Dollor.ai does not restrict your ability to use other TNC platforms
  • Dollor.ai does not restrict you from engaging in other commercial activities
  • You and Dollor.ai agree in writing that you are an independent contractor

4.2 Driver Requirements

To drive on the Platform, you must comply with Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-106:

Background Check Requirements:

  • Local and national criminal background check
  • Multistate criminal records database check
  • U.S. Department of Justice national sex offender registry search
  • Driving history review

You may NOT drive if you have:

  • Been convicted of a violent felony, sexual offense, or act of terror
  • More than three (3) moving violations in the past three (3) years
  • A DUI/DWI conviction within the past seven (7) years
  • Been convicted of driving without valid insurance

Dollor.ai charges the following distance-based Platform Fees pursuant to Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-103:

Tier Trip Distance Platform Fee Examples
Tier 1 0 - 9.99 miles $1.00 City trips, airport shuttles
Tier 2 10 - 24.99 miles $2.00 Suburban, inter-city
Tier 3 25+ miles $3.00 Long-distance, cross-county
