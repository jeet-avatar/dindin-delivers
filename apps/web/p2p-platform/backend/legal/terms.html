4. Driver-Partner Terms and Proposition 22

4.1 Independent Contractor Status (Proposition 22)

Pursuant to California Proposition 22 (Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 7448–7467), Driver-Partners using the Dollor.ai Platform are classified as independent contractors, not employees.

As an independent contractor under Proposition 22, Driver-Partners:

  • Set their own schedule and may log on or off at any time
  • May work for competing TNC platforms simultaneously
  • Are not subject to a minimum number of hours or trips
  • Are responsible for their own taxes and business expenses
  • Are entitled to certain Proposition 22 earnings and benefits guarantees (see Section 4.3)

4.2 Driver-Partner Eligibility Requirements

To become a Driver-Partner, you must:

  • Be at least 25 years of age (California TNC requirement)
  • Hold a valid California driver's license in good standing
  • Pass a criminal background check (California DOJ and FBI check)
  • Pass a California DMV motor vehicle record check (no more than 3 moving violations in 3 years; no DUI within 7 years)
  • Operate a qualifying vehicle (model year within 10 years, 4-door, passing safety inspection)
  • Maintain TNC-compliant personal auto insurance
  • Comply with all applicable California Vehicle Code requirements

4.3 Proposition 22 Earnings Guarantee

Dollor.ai provides the following Proposition 22 protections to California Driver-Partners:

  • Earnings guarantee: Net earnings of at least 120% of the California minimum wage for engaged time (Period 2 and 3)
  • Healthcare stipend: Quarterly stipend for Driver-Partners averaging 15+ hours/week of engaged time
  • Accident insurance: Occupational accident insurance covering injuries during app-on periods
  • Anti-discrimination protections: Consistent with the California Unruh Civil Rights Act

4.4 Zero Tolerance Policy (California Requirement)

Dollor.ai maintains a zero tolerance policy for drug and alcohol use, as required by California PUC § 5440:

  • Driver-Partners must not be under the influence of drugs or alcohol while the app is active
  • Riders may report suspected impairment via the in-app safety button
  • Reports are investigated within 24 hours; implicated drivers are immediately deactivated pending investigation
  • Confirmed violations result in permanent deactivation

4.5 Driver Deactivation and Appeals

Driver-Partners subject to deactivation have the right to:

  • Receive written notice of the reason for deactivation
  • Appeal the decision within 14 days to an independent arbitration panel
  • Continue using the Platform pending appeal, unless deactivation is for safety-related reasons

To initiate an appeal, contact drivers@dollor.ai within 14 days of receiving a deactivation notice.

5.1 Eligibility

  • Must be at least 18 years of age

  • Must provide accurate account information

5.2 Bidding and Fare Negotiation

Dollor.ai operates an open bidding marketplace:

  • After requesting a ride, Driver-Partners submit fare bids
  • Riders may accept a bid, counter-offer, or decline
  • All fees, including the platform service fee, are disclosed before confirmation

5.3 Rider Conduct

Riders agree to:

  • Not bring prohibited items (weapons, illegal substances) into vehicles

  • Not engage in fraudulent trip reports or chargebacks
