7.2 Refunds and Cancellations

Food Delivery Orders:

  • Before restaurant acceptance: Orders can be cancelled for a full refund at any time before the restaurant accepts and begins preparation.
  • After restaurant acceptance: Once a restaurant has accepted your order and begun preparation, the order cannot be cancelled through the app. You may request a refund by contacting support@dollor.ai.
  • Restaurant cancellation or timeout: If the restaurant declines your order or fails to respond within the acceptance window, your payment is automatically refunded in full.
  • Delivery failure: If your order is not delivered due to driver issues, you are eligible for a full refund.
  • Quality or accuracy issues: Report through the app or contact support@dollor.ai within 48 hours of delivery for review.

Rideshare:

  • Cancellation before driver assignment: No charge.
  • Cancellation after driver assignment and en route: A cancellation fee may apply.
  • Driver cancellation or no-show: No charge to rider.

All refunds are processed within 1-5 business days to the original payment method. For refund requests, email support@dollor.ai with your order/ride number.
