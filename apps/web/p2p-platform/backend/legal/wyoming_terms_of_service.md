## 2. DEFINITIONS

**"Digital Network"** means any online-enabled application, software, website, or system offered or used by Dollor.ai that enables riders to prearrange rides with drivers.

**"Driver"** means an independent contractor who: (i) has been authorized by Dollor.ai to access the Platform; (ii) uses a personal vehicle to provide prearranged rides to Riders; and (iii) receives compensation for providing such rides.

**"Personal Vehicle"** means a vehicle that is used by a Driver to provide prearranged rides and is: (i) owned, leased, or otherwise authorized for use by the Driver; and (ii) not a taxicab, limousine, for-hire vehicle, or commercial vehicle.

**"Prearranged Ride"** means transportation provided by a Driver to a Rider: (i) that begins when a Driver accepts a ride request through the Platform; (ii) continues while the Driver transports the Rider; and (iii) ends when the Rider exits the vehicle.

**"Platform Fee"** means the fee charged by Dollor.ai for facilitating the connection between Riders and Drivers through the Platform.

**"Rider"** means an individual who uses the Platform to request prearranged rides from Drivers.

## 4. DRIVER TERMS

### 4.1 Independent Contractor Status
Pursuant to Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-110, Drivers are independent contractors, NOT employees of Dollor.ai, when ALL of the following conditions are met:
- Dollor.ai does not prescribe specific hours during which you must be available
- Dollor.ai does not restrict your ability to use other TNC platforms
- Dollor.ai does not restrict you from engaging in other commercial activities
- You and Dollor.ai agree in writing that you are an independent contractor

**YOU ACKNOWLEDGE AND AGREE THAT YOU ARE AN INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR.**

### 4.2 Driver Requirements
To drive on the Platform, you must comply with Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-106:

**Background Check Requirements:**
- Local and national criminal background check
- Multistate criminal records database check
- U.S. Department of Justice national sex offender registry search
- Driving history review

**You may NOT drive if you have:**
- Been convicted of a violent felony, sexual offense, or act of terror
- More than three (3) moving violations in the past three (3) years
- A DUI/DWI conviction within the past seven (7) years
- Been convicted of driving without valid insurance

### 4.3 Vehicle Requirements
Your vehicle must:
- Be a personal vehicle (not a taxicab, limousine, or commercial vehicle)
- Meet Wyoming safety inspection requirements
- Be properly registered in Wyoming or your state of residence
- Not exceed the maximum age requirements (if any) set by Dollor.ai

### 4.4 Insurance Requirements
Pursuant to Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-107, you must maintain insurance coverage as follows:

**While Available (App On, Not Engaged in Ride):**
- $50,000 per person for death and bodily injury
- $100,000 per incident for death and bodily injury
- $25,000 for property damage

**While Engaged in Prearranged Ride:**
- $1,000,000 combined single limit for death, bodily injury, and property damage
- Uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage as required by W.S. 31-10-101

Dollor.ai maintains insurance coverage that may supplement your personal coverage. You are required to carry your own insurance that covers TNC driving activities.

### 4.5 Required Disclosures
Before you provide rides, you must disclose to your personal automobile insurer that you are a TNC driver, as your personal policy may not cover TNC activities.

### 4.6 Driver Identification
While engaged in TNC services, you must display:
- A Dollor.ai trade dress (logo/decal) as provided
- Information allowing Riders to identify your vehicle

### 4.7 Electronic Receipts
After each ride, Dollor.ai will provide an electronic receipt to the Rider showing:
- Origin and destination of the trip
- Total time and distance of the trip
- Itemized fare charged (transportation + Platform Fee)

### 4.8 Driver Earnings and Taxes
- You receive the fare minus the Platform Fee for each ride
- You are responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on your earnings
- Dollor.ai will issue IRS Form 1099-K if your earnings exceed the reporting threshold

## 5. PLATFORM FEES AND PAYMENTS

### 5.1 Fee Structure
Dollor.ai charges the following **distance-based** Platform Fees pursuant to Wyoming Statutes Section 31-20-103:

| Tier | Trip Distance | Platform Fee | Examples |
|------|--------------|--------------|----------|
| **Tier 1** | 0 - 9.99 miles | **$1.00** | City trips, airport shuttles |
| **Tier 2** | 10 - 24.99 miles | **$2.00** | Suburban, inter-city |
| **Tier 3** | 25+ miles | **$3.00** | Long-distance, cross-county |

### 5.2 Fee Calculation Example

**48-mile ride from Laramie to Cheyenne:**
```
Transportation Fare:
  Base Fare:              $2.50
  Distance (48 mi):      $55.20
  Time (52 min):          $9.36
  ─────────────────────────────
  Subtotal:              $67.06  ← Driver receives 100%

Platform Fee (Tier 3):    $3.00  ← Dollor.ai receives
  ─────────────────────────────
TOTAL CHARGED:           $70.06  ← Customer pays
```

### 5.3 Driver Earnings
- Drivers receive **100% of the transportation fare**
- Platform fee is charged separately to the customer
- Platform fee does NOT reduce driver earnings
- Tips go 100% to drivers (not subject to fees)

### 5.4 Fee Transparency (W.S. 31-20-103)
Per Wyoming law, before you confirm a ride:
- You will see the **itemized fare breakdown**
- Platform Fee is displayed **separately** from the fare
- You must **accept the total** before the ride is confirmed
- This information appears on your electronic receipt

### 5.5 Airport Fees
Additional fees apply at Wyoming airports:

| Airport | Code | Fee |
|---------|------|-----|
| Jackson Hole | JAC | $2.00 |
| Cheyenne Regional | CYS | $1.50 |
| Casper-Natrona | CPR | $1.50 |
| Riverton Regional | RIW | $1.00 |
| Sheridan County | SHR | $1.00 |

These fees are collected by Dollor.ai and remitted to airport authorities.

### 5.6 Payment Processing
- All payments are processed through Stripe, Inc.
- Payment is authorized **before** ride begins
- Driver payouts are processed weekly (every Monday)
- Dollor.ai does not store full payment card information
- PCI-DSS compliant payment handling

### 5.7 Cancellation Fees

| When Cancelled | Fee |
|----------------|-----|
| Before driver accepts | $0.00 (free) |
| After acceptance, before pickup | $3.00 |
| After pickup begins | Full fare charged |

Platform fees are refundable if the ride does not complete due to driver or platform issues.
