Refund Policy

Dollor.ai Peer-to-Peer Marketplace

Last Updated: January 2026
Applies to: All Dollor.ai platform transactions

Important: Dollor.ai is a peer-to-peer (P2P) matchmaking platform that connects customers with independent restaurants and driver partners. When you place an order or book a ride, your transaction is with those independent service providers, not with Dollor.ai directly. However, to ensure a positive experience, Dollor.ai facilitates the refund process on behalf of our platform participants.

1. Food Delivery Refunds

Full Refund Scenarios

  • Order never received: If your order is not delivered and cannot be located
  • Cancellation before restaurant acceptance: If you cancel before the restaurant has accepted your order. Once accepted, the order cannot be cancelled through the app.
  • Restaurant cancellation: If the restaurant cancels your order for any reason
  • Delivery failure: If your order was not delivered due to a driver issue and delivery cannot be completed
  • Food safety concern: If you receive food that poses a health risk
  • Completely wrong order: If you receive an entirely different order

Partial Refund Scenarios

  • Missing items: Refund for the specific items not received
  • Wrong items: Refund for incorrect items received
  • Quality issues: Partial refund if food quality does not meet reasonable expectations
  • Late cancellation: Refund minus preparation costs if cancelled after cooking begins

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after order is prepared and dispatched
  • Incorrect delivery address provided by customer
  • Customer not available at delivery location
  • Orders consumed before reporting an issue
  • Subjective taste preferences
  • Orders cancelled after the restaurant has accepted and begun preparation (partial refund may be available upon review)

2. Rideshare Refunds

Full Refund Scenarios

  • Driver no-show: If the driver fails to arrive at the pickup location
  • Cancellation before driver assigned: Full refund of any pre-charged amount
  • Safety incident: If the ride is terminated due to safety concerns
  • Technical error: If charged incorrectly due to app malfunction

Cancellation Fees

Rider Cancellation: A cancellation fee may apply if you cancel after a driver has been assigned and is en route.

Driver Cancellation: If a driver cancels after accepting your ride, no fee is charged to you.

3. Refund Timeline

Scenario Refund Amount Processing Time
Order cancelled before restaurant confirmation Full refund (100%) 1-3 business days
Order cancelled after preparation begins Partial refund 3-5 business days
Missing/wrong items Item value refund 1-3 business days
Driver no-show Full refund (100%) 1-3 business days
Ride cancelled before driver assigned Full refund (100%) 1-3 business days

Note: Credit card refunds may take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement.

4. How to Request a Refund

  1. Report the Issue: Open the Dollor.ai app and go to your order/ride history. Select the affected order and tap "Report Issue."
  2. Provide Details: Describe the issue and upload photos if applicable.
  3. Review Process: Our team will review your request within 24-48 hours.
  4. Resolution: You'll receive notification of the decision via email and in-app.

Time Limit: Refund requests must be submitted within 24 hours of order delivery or ride completion.

5. Platform Fees

  • Food Delivery: $1.00 flat fee per order
  • Rideshare: $1.00 - $3.00 tiered fee based on fare value

Platform fees are refunded when you receive a full refund for your order or ride.

6. Contact Us

For refund inquiries or assistance:

  • Email: support@dollor.ai
  • In-App: Help & Support > Refund Request
  • Response Time: Within 24 hours
