Dollor is the fairest delivery and rideshare app - order food from multiple restaurants or book a ride, all with transparent pricing where you see exactly where your money goes.

FOOD DELIVERY

Order from Multiple Restaurants:
- Add items from up to 3 restaurants in one order
- One driver, one delivery, one checkout
- Perfect for groups with different cravings

Fair for Everyone:
- Drivers keep 100% of delivery fees and tips
- Restaurants pay just $1 per order (no percentage cuts)
- You pay only $1 platform fee per restaurant

RIDESHARE

Peer-to-Peer Rides:
- Connect directly with independent drivers
- Drivers set their own rates
- 96% of your fare goes to the driver

Simple Pricing:
- $1-$3 tiered connection fee based on trip cost
- No surge pricing
- Transparent fare breakdown before you book

TRANSPARENT PRICING

See exactly where your money goes:
- Food cost goes 100% to restaurants
- Delivery fees and tips go 100% to drivers
- Platform fees clearly shown
- No hidden charges

FEATURES

- Apple Pay for fast, secure checkout
- Real-time order and ride tracking
- Save favorite restaurants and addresses
- Schedule orders and rides in advance
- AI Food Assistant to help you decide

HOW IT WORKS

Food Delivery:
1. Browse restaurants near you
2. Add items from up to 3 restaurants
3. Checkout with Apple Pay or card
4. Track your order in real-time

Rideshare:
1. Enter pickup and dropoff locations
2. See transparent fare breakdown
3. Find a driver and confirm
4. Track your ride in real-time
