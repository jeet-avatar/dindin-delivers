APP OVERVIEW:
Dollor is a matchmaking platform with two services:
1. Food Delivery - connecting customers with restaurants and delivery drivers
2. Rideshare - connecting riders with independent drivers

We are a matchmaking service, not a delivery company or transportation network company. Drivers are independent contractors who set their own rates.

KEY DIFFERENTIATORS:
- Drivers keep 100% of delivery fees and tips
- Transparent pricing shows exactly where money goes
- Multi-restaurant ordering (up to 3 restaurants per order)
- $1 flat platform fee per restaurant
- $1-$3 tiered rideshare connection fee
