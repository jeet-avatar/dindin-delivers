=== TESTING THE RIDER APP ===

1. SIGN IN
   - Open the app and tap "Sign In"
   - Use the demo credentials above (email + password login)
   - Do NOT use Google or Apple Sign-In for the demo account

2. REQUESTING A RIDE
   - On the home screen, tap "Ride" or "Book a Ride"
   - Enter a pickup address (e.g. "LAX Airport, Los Angeles CA")
   - Enter a dropoff address (e.g. "Santa Monica Pier, Santa Monica CA")
   - Tap "Get Fare Estimate" — an estimated fare will appear
   - Tap "Find Driver" to submit the ride request to nearby drivers
   - The app will show incoming bids from driver-partners
   - You can accept a bid, or counter-offer with a different amount

3. BIDDING FLOW
   - Drivers submit fare bids that appear on the Rider's screen
   - Rider can tap "Accept" to confirm the ride at that price
   - Rider can tap "Counter" to propose a different fare amount
   - Driver can accept the counter or counter back
   - Once both agree, the ride is confirmed and driver navigates to pickup

4. PAYMENT
   - A Visa card is pre-saved on this demo account — no card entry required
   - Tap "Find Driver" and the request will submit immediately
   - No real charges will be made in the review environment

5. FOUNDING MEMBER FEE
   - The optional $100 Founding Member fee is for real-world rideshare access
   - This is exempt from Apple IAP under App Store Review Guideline 3.1.3(a)
   - Skip this screen by navigating directly to "Book a Ride"
