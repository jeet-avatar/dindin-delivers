### Background Location Justification

```
Dollor Driver requires background location access for essential delivery functionality:

1. CUSTOMER TRANSPARENCY: Customers can track their delivery driver's location in real-time, which reduces customer anxiety and support calls asking "where is my order?"

2. NAVIGATION CONTINUITY: Drivers need turn-by-turn navigation to continue working even when the app is in the background (e.g., when receiving a phone call or checking messages).

3. DELIVERY VERIFICATION: Location is used to verify pickup and dropoff completion, ensuring accurate delivery records.

4. SAFETY: In case of emergencies or disputes, location history helps resolve issues and ensures driver safety.

Background location is ONLY active during an active delivery assignment. When no delivery is in progress, only foreground location is used. Users are clearly informed of this when enabling location permissions.

The app displays a visible indicator in the status bar when background location is active, and users can end tracking at any time by completing or canceling the delivery.
```
