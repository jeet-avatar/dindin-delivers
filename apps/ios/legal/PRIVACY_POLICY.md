## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption:** All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access, multi-factor authentication
- **Infrastructure:** Secure cloud hosting with AWS
- **Monitoring:** 24/7 security monitoring and intrusion detection
- **Audits:** Regular security assessments and penetration testing
- **Training:** Employee security awareness training

### 5.2 Password Security

- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
- We never store plaintext passwords
- Password reset requires email verification

### 5.3 Payment Security

- PCI DSS compliant payment processing
- Card details handled exclusively by Stripe
- We never store full card numbers

### 5.4 Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information:
- We will notify you within 72 hours
- We will notify relevant authorities as required by law
- We will provide guidance on protective steps

**Right to Opt-Out:**

**Right to Non-Discrimination:**
- We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

**Shine the Light:**

To exercise California rights, contact: privacy@dollor.ai

### 12.2 Nevada Privacy Rights

Nevada residents may opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not currently sell personal information as defined under Nevada law.

### 12.3 European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

- Contract performance (providing Services)
- Legitimate interests (improving Services, security)
- Legal compliance
- Consent (marketing communications)

**Additional Rights:**
- Right to restriction of processing
- Right to object to processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to withdraw consent
- Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

**Data Controller:**
Dollor AI Service is the data controller for your personal information.

### 12.4 Location Data Disclosure

**When Location is Collected:**
- During active food orders (for delivery tracking)
- During active rideshare trips
- When browsing nearby restaurants (with permission)

**How to Control Location:**
- iOS: Settings > Privacy > Location Services > Dollor
- Options: Never, While Using, Always

**Impact of Disabling:**
- Core features may not function without location access
- You may still use pickup orders without location

### 12.5 Biometric Data

We do not collect biometric data such as fingerprints or facial recognition data.

### 12.6 Voice Data

- Transcribed text may be used for search queries

### 12.7 Cookies and Tracking

Our mobile apps do not use cookies. We use:
- Device identifiers for analytics
- Advertising identifiers (with consent)

To reset advertising identifier:
- iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking
