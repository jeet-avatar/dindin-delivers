=== TESTING THE DRIVER APP ===

1. SIGN IN
   - Open the app and tap "Driver Login"
   - Use email + password login with the demo credentials above
   - The demo driver account is pre-approved — no document upload required

2. GOING ONLINE
   - After sign-in, you will see the driver dashboard
   - Tap "Go Online" to become available for ride requests
   - The app will show your current location on the map

3. RECEIVING A RIDE REQUEST
   - When a rider submits a request nearby, you will receive a notification
   - Tap the notification to view the ride request details (pickup, dropoff, distance)
   - Tap "Submit Bid" to enter your fare amount
   - The rider may accept, decline, or counter-offer

4. BIDDING FLOW
   - You submit a bid with your desired fare
   - If the rider counters, you will see their counter-offer amount
   - You can accept the rider's counter or propose a new amount
   - Once both agree, the app navigates you to the pickup location

5. TO TEST END-TO-END (with Rider app simultaneously):
   - Open the Rider app (Dollor) on a second device or simulator
   - Log in as review.rider@dollor.ai / DollorReview2026!
   - Submit a ride request — it will appear in the Driver app for bidding

6. BACKGROUND LOCATION
   - The driver app requires location access "Always" for period 1 (waiting for rides)
   - This is required for California CPUC TNC compliance and is disclosed in the Privacy Policy
