Drive with Dollor — California's open-bid rideshare platform where you set your price. Submit bids to riders, negotiate fares, and earn more per mile than any other rideshare platform.

HOW IT WORKS

You're in Control:
- Go online when you want, go offline when you don't
- Browse open ride requests near you
- Submit your own fare bid — no algorithm-forced pricing
- Counter-offer if a rider negotiates — you decide what's worth your time

Keep More of What You Earn:
- You keep the full fare you negotiate
- 100% of tips go directly to you
- Platform fee is a small flat amount, not a percentage of your earnings
- Weekly payouts directly to your bank account

CALIFORNIA PROPOSITION 22 PROTECTIONS

As a Dollor Driver-Partner, you are an independent contractor under California Proposition 22 with guaranteed protections:
- Earnings guarantee: 120% of California minimum wage for engaged time
- Quarterly healthcare stipend (for 15+ hours/week average)
- Occupational accident insurance while the app is active
- Freedom to work for other platforms simultaneously

SAFETY BUILT IN

- In-app navigation powered by Google Maps
- Voice assistant for hands-free operation while driving
- Two-way rating system — only verified riders book trips
- 24/7 support from the Dollor driver team
- Zero tolerance for harassment — all incidents reviewed within 24 hours

REQUIREMENTS TO DRIVE

To become a Dollor Driver-Partner in California you must:
- Be at least 25 years of age
- Hold a valid California driver's license
- Pass a criminal background check (CA DOJ + FBI) and DMV record check
- Have a 4-door vehicle from the last 10 model years
- Carry TNC-eligible auto insurance

ABOUT DOLLOR

Dollor is a California-licensed Transportation Network Company (TNC) operating under CPUC regulations, based in Rancho Santa Margarita, CA.
