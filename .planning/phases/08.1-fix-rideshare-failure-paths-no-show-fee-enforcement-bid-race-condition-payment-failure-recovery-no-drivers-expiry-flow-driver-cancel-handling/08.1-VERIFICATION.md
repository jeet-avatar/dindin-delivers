## Summary

Phase 08.1 goal is fully achieved. All three failure paths are now handled:

1. **No-show fee (Plan 01):** `mark_passenger_no_show()` now cancels the pre-auth PaymentIntent, charges the customer $5.00 off-session using their default saved card, and transfers $4.00 to the driver via Stripe Connect. All three Stripe calls use ride-scoped idempotency keys. Missing card or Stripe errors degrade to `payment_status='manual_review'` without blocking the no-show record.

2. **Payment capture retry (Plan 02):** `complete_ride()` now sets `payment_status='capture_failed'` instead of silently swallowing capture exceptions. A `check_capture_retry_job()` background function retries failed captures every 5 minutes with a hard ceiling of 3 attempts (`payment_retry_count` column tracks this). The driver payout is guarded — it is skipped when capture failed to avoid issuing a Transfer against a failed charge. Alembic migration adds the column properly.

3. **Expiry flow iOS banner (Plan 03):** `RideRequestView.swift` now observes the `RideRequestExpired` NotificationCenter event (already posted by `eatfaircustomerApp.swift`) and shows an orange banner with a "Try Again" button that calls `viewModel.resetRide()`. The banner auto-dismisses after 10 seconds. Human-verified on simulator.
