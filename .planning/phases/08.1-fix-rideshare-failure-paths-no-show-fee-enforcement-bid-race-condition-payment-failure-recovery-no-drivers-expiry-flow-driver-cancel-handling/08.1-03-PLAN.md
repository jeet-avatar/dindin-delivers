<objective>
Wire the existing `RideRequestExpired` push notification into a visible customer-facing banner in RideRequestView.

Purpose: The backend scheduler sends a `ride_expired` push when no driver accepts within the bidding window. The push handler in `eatfaircustomerApp.swift` already posts a `RideRequestExpired` NotificationCenter event, but nothing in the app listens to it — customers tap the notification and nothing happens, leaving them stranded with no call to action.

Output: `RideRequestView.swift` gains an `onReceive` handler for `RideRequestExpired` that shows an in-app banner "No drivers accepted your request. Try again?" with a "Try Again" button that resets the booking form so the customer can rebook immediately.
</objective>
