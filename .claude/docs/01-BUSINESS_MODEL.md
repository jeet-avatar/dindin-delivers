# Dollor.ai Business Model

> **Last Updated:** December 2025

---

## Critical Legal Distinction

Dollor.ai operates as a **MATCHMAKING SERVICE**, NOT a delivery company or transportation network company (TNC). This positioning provides legal protection and avoids extensive licensing requirements.

---

## Matchmaking Service Definition

### What We Are
- Technology matchmaking platform
- Connection facilitator
- Payment processor (pass-through)
- Software-as-a-Service provider

### What We Are NOT
- Delivery company
- Transportation network company (TNC)
- Employer of drivers
- Food service provider

### We Match
- Hungry customers with Restaurants
- Food orders with Independent delivery partners
- Riders with Independent driver partners

---

## Pricing Model - Flat Fee Structure

Simple, transparent, fair pricing with no percentage-based commissions.

### Food Delivery Matchmaking

| Party | Fee | Description |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| **Customer** | $1.00 per order | Matchmaking fee |
| **Restaurant** | $1.00 per order | Platform listing fee |
| **Driver** | $0.00 | FREE - no commission |
| **Tips** | $0.00 | 100% go to driver |

**Platform Revenue:** $2.00 per order

### Rideshare Matchmaking (Tiered)

| Fare Range | Platform Fee |
|------------|--------------|
| $0 - $35 | $1.00 |
| $35 - $70 | $2.00 |
| $70+ | $3.00 |

### Why This Model Works
- Simple and transparent pricing
- Drivers keep 100% of delivery fees + tips
- No "gig economy exploitation" criticism
- Lower barrier for restaurants

## Legal Protections

| Protection | Implementation |
|------------|----------------|
| Independent Contractor Status | Drivers set own hours, accept/decline freely |
| No Route Control | Drivers choose their own routes |
| Pass-Through Payments | We process payments, not handle cash |
| Flat Fee Model | No commission = less employer risk |
| Terms of Service | Clear matchmaking language |
