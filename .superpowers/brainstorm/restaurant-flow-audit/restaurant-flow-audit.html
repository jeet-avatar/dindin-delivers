PAYMENT & PAYOUT FLOW

Fee Flow for a Delivered Food Order

Customer pays: subtotal + tax + delivery_fee + tip + $1 service fee (Stripe PaymentIntent)
↓ confirm-payment endpoint → Stripe capture → PENDING_RESTAURANT
↓ ... lifecycle stages ...
↓ DELIVERED event (order_flow.py:3974-4050)
Platform keeps: $1 (from customer service fee) + $1 (from restaurant) = $2 total per order
Restaurant receives: subtotal − discount_absorbed − $1 platform_fee → VendorPayout record (status: pending)
Driver receives: delivery_fee (100%) + tip (100%) → DriverPayout record (status: pending)
⚠ Stripe transfer to vendor/driver NOT auto-triggered at delivery — payout batch runs separately
⚠ Unlike rideshare (which auto-transfers), food payout batch must be triggered manually or via scheduler
Source: order_flow.py:400-430 (fee model), order_flow.py:3974-4050 (accounting at delivery)
Platform fee model: $1 flat per party — NOT a % commission (rideshare_payments.py:36)
JournalEntry created at DELIVERED · VendorPayout + DriverPayout created (pending)
PartyAmountWhen TriggeredHow
Customer subtotal + tax + $1 + delivery_fee + tip At checkout (Stripe PI creation) Stripe PaymentIntent capture
Platform $2 total ($1 from customer + $1 from restaurant) At DELIVERED event JournalEntry + accounting split
Restaurant subtotal − discount_absorbed − $1 At DELIVERED — payout record created VendorPayout (pending) → batch transfer
Driver delivery_fee (100%) + tip (100%) At DELIVERED — payout record created DriverPayout (pending) → batch transfer
